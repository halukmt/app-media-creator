# Light humanizer pass for long store texts

Use this pass on the promotional text, the descriptions and what's new, after writing them. Never on the name,
subtitle, title or keywords. Keep every fact: an added or lost fact is an error. Work in each language of the job.

Adapted from the Light mode of [ste80-humanizer](https://github.com/Dodaios/ste80-humanizer) (MIT, © 2026 Dodaios), whose
catalogue of AI writing patterns is adapted from [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) (MIT, © 2025
Siqi Chen). Licence texts: `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` of app-media-creator.

## 1. Remove the AI tells

| Tell | Watch for | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Not X but Y | "not just X, but Y", "it's not X, it's Y" | State the point. |
| Closers and fragments | "That's the real win.", "No setup. No config." | Cut a closer that repeats; merge fragments into one claim. |
| Sayings that sound deep | "at its core", "X is the Y of Z" | Say the specific thing. |
| Staged run-up | "Here's the thing", "Let's dive in" | Start with the point. |
| Forced triads | "fast, reliable and powerful" | Keep three only when there are three facts. |
| Dashes everywhere | em dash (—), en dash (–) | Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses. |
| Stacked qualifiers | "could potentially", "might arguably" | Keep one, only when it is true. |
| Overused AI words | seamless, robust, leverage, elevate, unlock, empower, effortless, game-changer, cutting-edge | Use the plain word. |
| Inflated significance | "a new era", "transforms the way you" | Keep the fact; end on the last concrete fact. |
| Sales language | stunning, world-class, revolutionary | Say what the app is and does. |
| Borrowed authority | "experts agree", "loved by thousands" | Cut it, or name the source when it is real and an allowed claim. |
| "Serves as" | serves as, boasts, features | Use is, are, has. |
| Decoration | bold labels on every line, emoji as bullets, Title Case | Plain lines; sentence case. |
| Chatbot residue | "I hope this helps", "Let me know" | Remove. |

Most of these words and patterns have equivalents in other languages (German "nahtlos", "mühelos"; Turkish "sorunsuz",
"zahmetsiz"); remove those too.

## 2. Fix only the longest sentences

From the structure rules, fix only sentences with more than about 30 words or with three ideas: split them into one
idea per sentence. Use active voice and name who acts ("You pick the players", not "Players are picked"). Vary sentence
length a little; a row of equally short sentences sounds mechanical too.

## 3. Check

Compare the rewrite with your fact list: nothing added, nothing lost. Then run the skill's check script again.
